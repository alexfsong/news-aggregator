## Claude Sets New Record on 37-Year-Old Math Benchmark After 650 Failed Attempts

Anthropic published research showing Claude solved a long-standing mathematical problem related to the Riemann Zeta function faster than any prior method, setting a record on a benchmark that has stood for 37 years. The model required roughly 650 attempts before producing a correct solution—illustrating that success came through scale of sampling rather than single-shot reasoning. Two Minute Papers covered the story noting the achievement is real but bounded: Scientific American clarified that this is not a solution to the Riemann Hypothesis, which remains open; Claude found an improved bound or algorithmic result in a related subproblem.

**Why it matters:** Even with heavy sampling, a frontier LLM achieving a novel mathematical result that eluded humans for decades suggests that AI-assisted formal reasoning is moving from proof-checking toward discovery—with implications for competitive mathematics and scientific research.
