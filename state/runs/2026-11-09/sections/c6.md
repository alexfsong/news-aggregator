## AI Is Now Meaningfully Accelerating Its Own Research

A widely-read Substack post ("AI is dramatically accelerating AI research," 374 HN points) documented concrete evidence that AI tools are shortening research iteration cycles across alignment, ML theory, and engineering — not just writing boilerplate, but participating in hypothesis generation. Two Minute Papers covered two notable OpenAI results: "Weight Space Programming," which lets models modify their own weights directly (described as "opening the black box"), and a claimed proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false — a decades-old open problem in complexity theory — generated with AI assistance. OpenAI separately published a post on how ChatGPT and Codex are accelerating CAD, PCB design, and simulation workflows for physical-world engineering.

**Why it matters:** If AI is genuinely shortening the loop between research idea and experimental validation, capability jumps could arrive faster than current scaling forecasts suggest — compressing the timeline for both breakthroughs and associated risks.
