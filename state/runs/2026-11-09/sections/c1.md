## Google DeepMind Launches Gemini 4 Carbon and Genie 4 World Simulator

Google DeepMind this fortnight unveiled two flagship models: Gemini 4 Carbon, described as its "next frontier model," and Genie 4, a world simulation engine promising "unprecedented realism in real-time simulation." A subsequent Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash release (Nov 5) added a faster, cost-effective multimodal variant with extended video-clip support. The same week, DeepMind published Google Antigravity, an agentic cheminformatics tool that searches compound libraries and proposes novel drug candidates. AI Explained noted that the Carbon launch coincided with both Altman and Amodei suggesting they may ship models whose behavior they "cannot fully verify."

**Why it matters:** Google is pushing on three fronts simultaneously — frontier language, world simulation, and scientific agents — signalling that the race for general-purpose AI is intensifying across modalities, not just text.
