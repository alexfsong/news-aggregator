## Trump Plans to Ban the Term "Artificial Intelligence" in Favour of "Super Intelligence"

Reuters, The Hill, and Forbes all confirmed that President Trump announced plans to formally ban the use of the term "artificial intelligence" in government documents, pushing instead for "Super Intelligence" as the preferred label. Simon Willison traced the policy context: US Census Bureau data had shown corporate AI adoption trending downward in 2025, and the rebranding appears aimed at recasting AI policy as an American leadership project. MIT Technology Review connected the move to broader questions about how to frame AI governance — noting that "reverse game theory" approaches for controlling capable AI agents are gaining attention precisely as the definitional landscape shifts.

**Why it matters:** Renaming a technology in official policy vocabulary can reshape regulation, procurement, and public discourse, making it harder to apply existing AI safety frameworks that use the displaced terminology.
