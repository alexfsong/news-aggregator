## Claude Fable 5.1 Launches, but Developers Hit Rate Ceilings Anthropic Didn't Warn About

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 on September 1, touting them as significantly more capable while promising 20x higher usage limits for developers. The Fable model is the flagship; Two Minute Papers describes it as "much stranger than the headlines suggest," pointing to behaviors in the technical report that differ markedly from earlier Claude versions. However, within days of launch, The New Stack reported that developers were hitting undisclosed weekly ceilings — a sharp discrepancy from Anthropic's pre-launch messaging. Meanwhile, Fable 5.1 was quietly added to Claude for Government on September 10, and Anthropic published a companion post on "Developing Enterprise Frontier Safeguards" describing a tiered approach to capability release with enterprise customers.

**Why it matters:** The rate-ceiling controversy, arriving immediately after a 20x usage promise, signals that Anthropic is still managing supply constraints on its newest models — and that developer trust may hinge as much on accurate capacity communication as on raw capability.
