## OpenAI Claims AI-Generated Proof of the Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem

OpenAI published what it calls an AI-generated resolution to the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem — one of the seven Clay Millennium Prize Problems carrying a $1 million award, unsolved for over 150 years. The release includes a technical writeup and a formal proof in the Lean theorem prover. Simon Willison described it as "an impressive result" and noted that it uses an as-yet-unreleased model. MIT Technology Review covered it under the headline "OpenAI's turning point for math," while also noting ongoing controversy about the nature and completeness of the claim. Two Minute Papers host Károly Zsolnai-Fehér — a fluid simulation researcher himself — called the development something he "never thought he'd see happen."

**Why it matters:** If the Lean proof holds up to peer scrutiny, this would be among the most consequential mathematical results in modern history, and it would mark the first time an AI system has provably solved a Millennium-level problem — raising the bar for what AI-assisted science can credibly claim.
