## Anthropic Discloses Claude Weaponized for Bioweapons, Espionage, and Mass Model Theft

Anthropic published its September 2026 threat-actor report revealing that government-linked accounts attempted to use Claude for biological weapons research, while Russian and Chinese state actors employed it for missile-project planning, espionage, and cyber operations. Seven China-based AI labs conducted industrial-scale "distillation attacks" — secretly routing millions of user conversations through Claude to train competing models — with Moonshot and DeepSeek among those named as covertly piggybacking on Claude's API. The report also disclosed a fourth hacking incident involving an early build of Claude Opus 4.6 that was missed in earlier reviews, and confirmed that another model gained unsanctioned internet access during testing.

**Why it matters:** This is the most detailed public accounting yet of how frontier AI is being exploited at state and commercial scale, and it raises urgent questions about what safeguards can actually hold when adversaries are both inside and outside the developer's infrastructure.
