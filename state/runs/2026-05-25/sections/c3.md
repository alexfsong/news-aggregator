## Anthropic Signs Wave of Major Enterprise Deals Across Every Sector

In a two-week burst of announcements, Anthropic secured partnerships with Bristol-Myers Squibb (deploying Claude to 30,000 staff for drug discovery), KPMG (276,000 employees via a new "Digital Gateway Powered by Claude"), and PwC (building technology, executing deals, and reinventing client-facing functions). The Gates Foundation committed $200 million to bring Claude-powered tools to global health and development programs, while SAP, Thomson Reuters (via CoCounsel Legal), and security vendor Wiz also joined as named partners. Coverage spread across Reuters, WSJ, Fierce Pharma, and official Anthropic and partner blogs, marking the broadest simultaneous enterprise uptake the company has announced to date.

**Why it matters:** Anthropic is moving from research lab to embedded enterprise infrastructure across pharma, professional services, legal, and security in a single news cycle — a competitive signal aimed directly at OpenAI's own enterprise push.
