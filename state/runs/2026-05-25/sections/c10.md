## Pope Leo XIV Publishes First Papal AI Encyclical Today, Co-Launched with Anthropic Co-Founder

Pope Leo XIV is publishing his first encyclical today (May 25), focused entirely on artificial intelligence — the first papal document dedicated to the topic. The Vatican separately established an Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence in the weeks prior. PBS reported that the encyclical launch will involve Anthropic co-founder Dario or Daniela Amodei, an unusual step that positions the Catholic Church's formal AI ethics framework alongside a leading AI safety lab. OSV News previewed Leo's prior statements emphasizing both the promise and the moral responsibility of AI development.

**Why it matters:** A papal encyclical carries binding moral weight for 1.4 billion Catholics and shapes global policy discussions; its publication today — on the day this digest is issued — marks a significant moment in the governance and ethics conversation around AI.
