## Salesforce and Anthropic Launch "Claudeforce" Integrated CRM-AI Product

Salesforce and Anthropic deepened their partnership this week with a jointly branded product called "Claudeforce," positioned as combining the top-ranked AI CRM with Claude. The announcement came alongside Salesforce's quarterly earnings, which showed rising profit and revenue, suggesting the AI partnership is material to Benioff's growth story. CNBC reported Benioff used the occasion to address "SaaSpocalypse" concerns — the fear that agentic AI will cannibalize Salesforce's own software business — by framing the Claude integration as Salesforce's answer rather than a threat.

**Why it matters:** The Claudeforce branding signals a shift from API-level AI integrations toward co-branded enterprise products, which could reshape how both vendors compete with Microsoft Copilot.
