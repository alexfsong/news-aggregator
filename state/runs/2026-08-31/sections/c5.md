## ChatGPT Work Gains Enterprise Traction with Admin Tools and Case Studies

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work in July and has been iterating rapidly on it. Simon Willison's deep-dive explains it is actually two distinct products: a cloud-based version accessible through chatgpt.com, and a desktop-app version (formerly called Codex) that can access local files and run code. OpenAI shipped an Admin plugin this week for workspace-level management of members, permissions, and usage. Enterprise case studies are multiplying: Asana replaced five years of backlogged engineering work in two weeks for roughly $12K using Codex; Stampli cut launch production hours by 68%; NVIDIA and loveholidays reported similar productivity gains.

**Why it matters:** The gap between AI's productivity claims and documented enterprise results is narrowing — concrete cycle-time numbers from named companies are more durable evidence than capability benchmarks.
