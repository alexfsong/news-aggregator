## DeepSeek V4 Pro and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next Compress the Open-Closed AI Gap

Two Minute Papers covered three related developments this fortnight: DeepSeek's release of what the channel describes as a system that "shouldn't be possible" (with an accompanying technical report and Harness benchmark suite), the follow-on DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 model available on Hugging Face, and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, which a new technical report claims achieves performance comparable to much larger closed models on a consumer RTX 3090. Together these point to a pattern: open or low-cost Chinese lab releases are consistently closing the quality gap with frontier closed models within weeks of each new capability appearing.

**Why it matters:** If the cost-performance gap between frontier closed models and open alternatives keeps compressing, the pricing leverage of labs like OpenAI and Anthropic narrows materially.
