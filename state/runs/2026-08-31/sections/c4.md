## Anthropic Extends Claude Into Physical AI for Science and Manufacturing

Anthropic announced its first move into physical AI, releasing a framework that lets scientists and manufacturers connect Claude to laboratory instruments, robots, and other physical equipment. Anthropic's own blog highlights use cases in protein design and analytical chemistry, and also previewed a "Model Hardware Standard" — apparently a specification for how AI models communicate with attached hardware. Bloomberg reported Claude was tested with robots and scientific lab tools; Fortune described this as Anthropic's entry into a domain previously dominated by robotics-focused AI labs.

**Why it matters:** Connecting frontier LLMs directly to lab instruments could compress experiment cycles in drug discovery and materials science, markets where Anthropic has been making targeted inroads.
