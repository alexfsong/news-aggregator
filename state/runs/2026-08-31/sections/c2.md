## Federal Judge Rules Pentagon Blacklisting of Anthropic Illegal

A US federal judge struck down the Department of Defense's blacklisting of Anthropic this week, calling it "illegal and baseless." The Pentagon had placed Anthropic on a restricted list, effectively barring it from certain defense contracts; the court found no lawful basis for that action. Reuters published a timeline of how the conflict escalated before reaching the courts. Anthropic can now pursue government business without that restriction in place.

**Why it matters:** A ruling like this sets a precedent that federal agencies cannot arbitrarily block AI companies from defense work without clear statutory justification.
