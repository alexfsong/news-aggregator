## AI Tackles Biology and Astronomy: Sky Maps, DNA Prediction, and Scientific Skepticism

Two significant AI-in-science stories ran in parallel this week. Anthropic published a blog post claiming "Claude Science" produced the first complete map of the sky in ultraviolet light, while CNN reported that scientists are skeptical of a separate Anthropic-attributed biological discovery, noting it has not been independently replicated. On the DeepMind side, a Two Minute Papers video covered AlphaGenome Atlas — a model that predicts the effects of every possible single-letter DNA change in the human genome — marking a step toward understanding genetic variation at scale. A $1.8 billion global commitment to "AI-ready biological data" was also announced via the Biohub Virtual Biology Initiative expansion, signaling large-scale infrastructure investment to support AI-driven life sciences research.

**Why it matters:** The gap between Anthropic's self-reported AI science breakthroughs and the skeptical response from independent scientists highlights a pattern of premature claims in AI-for-science that could erode trust in legitimate advances like AlphaGenome.
