## Anthropic Offers Free Startup Year, Then Pauses Program Three Days Later

Anthropic launched an expanded Claude Startups program on October 6, offering eligible startups a free year of Claude Team plus $1,000 in API credits — reported by both CNBC and TechCrunch as a bid to attract founders away from OpenAI and other providers. By October 9, just three days after the announcement, Forbes reported that Anthropic had paused the free startup perks, citing undisclosed reasons. No explanation for the suspension was publicly provided.

**Why it matters:** Abruptly pulling a widely-publicized founder program days after launch is an unusual stumble for a company preparing for an IPO, and may reflect either demand that overwhelmed capacity or internal second-guessing of the economics.
