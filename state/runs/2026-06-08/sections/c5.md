## Trump signs new AI order, plans profit-share talks with AI firms

Less than two weeks after rescinding his previous AI executive order, President Trump signed a new one — "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" — and reactions split along predictable lines: the Consumer Bankers Association welcomed it as pro-innovation, while the Center for a New American Security flagged it as a notable marker for security-policy watchers. Politico reports Trump plans to meet with AI companies as soon as next week to discuss a government profit-share plan, suggesting the administration wants a direct financial stake in the AI boom rather than just rules to govern it. Bloomberg Law used the moment to argue the U.S. urgently needs one coherent national AI framework instead of the current patchwork of state and federal actions — underscoring how unsettled the regulatory picture remains even after the new order.

**Why it matters:** A government actively negotiating a profit share with AI companies, on top of rapid-fire executive orders, would mark a real shift from regulator to stakeholder in the industry's growth.
