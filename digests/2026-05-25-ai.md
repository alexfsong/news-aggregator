## Google I/O Launches Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini Omni, and Antigravity Platform

At Google I/O (May 20), Google shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash directly to general availability — skipping preview — and deployed it across the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, the Gemini API, and enterprise products. The same keynote introduced Gemini Omni (a multimodal flagship), Google Antigravity 2.0 (an agent-first development platform), and Project Genie (a Street View-powered world simulator now in Google AI Ultra). DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis described the current moment as "standing in the foothills of the singularity." Simon Willison noted that most other announcements were "coming soon" rather than available, while MIT Technology Review flagged Hassabis's rhetoric as a meaningful escalation in how labs frame the timeline to transformative AI.

**Why it matters:** Gemini 3.5 Flash going straight to GA — at higher cost than its predecessor but with agentic capabilities — is the model Google is betting its entire product stack on, making this a direct confrontation with OpenAI's GPT-5.5 in the enterprise agent market.

## Pope Leo XIV Publishes First Papal AI Encyclical Today, Co-Launched with Anthropic Co-Founder

Pope Leo XIV is publishing his first encyclical today (May 25), focused entirely on artificial intelligence — the first papal document dedicated to the topic. The Vatican separately established an Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence in the weeks prior. PBS reported that the encyclical launch will involve Anthropic co-founder Dario or Daniela Amodei, an unusual step that positions the Catholic Church's formal AI ethics framework alongside a leading AI safety lab. OSV News previewed Leo's prior statements emphasizing both the promise and the moral responsibility of AI development.

**Why it matters:** A papal encyclical carries binding moral weight for 1.4 billion Catholics and shapes global policy discussions; its publication today — on the day this digest is issued — marks a significant moment in the governance and ethics conversation around AI.

## NVIDIA's Nemotron-Labs Diffusion Models Generate Text Up to 6× Faster Than Autoregressive LLMs

NVIDIA released Nemotron-Labs Diffusion — a family of 3B, 8B, and 14B text models (plus an 8B vision-language model) that combine autoregressive and diffusion generation in a single checkpoint. Instead of producing one token at a time, the models fill 32-token blocks in parallel, then iteratively refine them. In self-speculation mode, the same model drafts bidirectionally and verifies causally, reaching ~865 tokens/second on B200 hardware — roughly 6× faster than a comparable autoregressive baseline — while remaining lossless vs. AR at temperature 0. The models are available under a commercially-friendly NVIDIA license; training code ships through the Megatron Bridge framework. Two Minute Papers separately covered DeepSeek's latest model under a similar efficiency headline the same week.

**Why it matters:** If diffusion-style decoding delivers 4–6× throughput at comparable accuracy, it reshapes the economics of inference at scale and could pressure the market for dedicated inference hardware and cloud compute pricing.
